# Commercial Officer Manual
**SWUWS Collection & Financial Governance Platform v1.0.0**

## 1. Issuing Receipts
- **Search**: Enter the customer's Account Number or Name in the search bar.
- **Select**: Choose the correct customer to load their active bills.
- **Record**: Enter the payment amount, method (Cash, MM, etc.), and reference number.
- **Print**: Generate the official receipt for the customer.

## 2. Searching Receipts
- Use the **Dashboard** or **Customers** tab to find previously issued receipts.
- Note the **Reconciliation Status** (Matched vs Pending) to ensure your banking has been confirmed by Finance.

## 3. Daily Totals
- View "Today's Cash Receipts" on your personal dashboard to verify your total collection before handover.
